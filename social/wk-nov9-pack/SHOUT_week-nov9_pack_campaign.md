# Shout hand-post pack: week of Nov 9–15, 2026

Rebuilt Nov 8, 2026, because the first build was never saved. Approved on HQ (card `ap-shout-pack-nov9`).
Metricool's free plan is used up for November, so you post these by hand in the apps' own free schedulers. That costs nothing.

- **Instagram + Facebook:** Meta Business Suite → Planner → Create post. Pick the account, upload the slides in order, paste the caption and set the time. Turn on the AI label ("Made with AI") where it's offered.
- **TikTok:** in the TikTok app, tap + → Photo, then pick the slides in order. Add a trending sound, paste the caption and turn on "AI-generated content" under More options. Post at the time below, or schedule it in TikTok Studio on desktop.
- Images are in this folder, one subfolder per post (`slide-1.png`, `slide-2.png`, …). On GitHub, open a slide and use "Download raw file".
- Prices and the sale were checked against CLAUDE.md and HQ on Nov 8. The 25% launch sale covers the Nice List Kit, Christmas Eve Mega Bundle, Wish List and Scavenger Hunt, and runs through Wed, Nov 18. Shirts and posters are **not** on sale.
- Once you've made Etsy Share & Save links, swap them in for the Facebook links below.

- **TikTok needs JPEG (Archie, Nov 10).** TikTok Business photo posts reject PNG. Each TikTok post folder has `slide-N.jpg` copies, so upload those, not the PNGs.
