# Shout: reply templates for comments and DMs

Shout's Chrome job uses these to draft replies (`docs/shout-chrome-posting.md`, step 6). The owner sends them. Keep each reply short and personal: use the person's first word or emoji back to them, and answer the real question. No made-up facts, discounts or promises.

| They say | Draft reply |
|---|---|
| Compliment ("so cute!", ❤️) | "Thank you so much! 🎄 Which page would your little one love most?" |
| "Where can I buy it?" / "Link?" | "Thank you! It's in our Etsy shop, MilestoneAndMeaning. The link is in our bio 🎁" (Facebook: paste the listing link from the post.) |
| "How does it work?" (printables) | "It's an instant download: type their name into the PDF, print at home and cut. About five minutes, and nothing ships 😊" |
| Paper or size question | "Every page comes in US Letter and A4. Cardstock looks lovely, but regular printer paper works too." |
| Price or sale question | Quote only the live price and sale dates from CLAUDE.md (25% launch sale through Nov 18; Cyber Specials Nov 23–Dec 1 on the printables, no shirts). |
| Shirt sizes or shipping | "Sizes are on the Etsy listing, and it's printed to order and shipped to you. Order by Dec 8 for Christmas delivery." (Check the cutoff in CLAUDE.md first.) |
| Golf print question | "It's a printed poster with free shipping, as a classic print or a wide panorama. The sizes are on the listing." |
| Tagged photo of our product | "This made our day! Thank you for sharing 🥹🎄 Can we repost it to our Stories?" |
| Order problem or complaint | Don't draft a public answer. Draft: "So sorry about that! Please message us on Etsy so we can sort it out right away." Flag it to the owner. |
| Spam, rude, or "promote your page" | Skip. No reply. |

Rules: no comment-keyword CTAs ("comment WORD"), since no auto-DM tool is set up. Never share personal details. Reply within 24 hours where possible, because fast replies help reach.
