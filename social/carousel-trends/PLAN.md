# Carousel trends: Milestone & Meaning holiday plan

Seven viral carousel formats, adapted to our Christmas printables. The first two are built (PNG slides at 1080x1350, 4:5) and ready to post. Upload slides in order as one Instagram/Facebook carousel or TikTok photo post.

Re-render after editing copy: `node social/carousel-trends/src/render.js`

---

## ✅ BUILT: "Follow the Line" (`follow-the-line/`, 4 slides)

Pick an emoji (🍪 🦌 🔑 ✈️) and follow its colored line across the swipes. Each one ends on a bundle item: cookie thank-you note, reindeer food tags, magic key, boarding pass.

**Caption:**
> Pick one and follow the line 👀 Which did you land on? Tell us in the comments 👇
> All four are in our Christmas Eve Bundle. Type their name, print at home, done. Link in bio.
>
> #christmasevebox #christmaseve #christmasprintables #santakey #christmastraditions #kidschristmas #momhacks #printables #etsyfinds

## ✅ BUILT: "Santa, You Didn't Pay Us" (`santa-didnt-pay/`, 5 slides)

The "fake client on blast" format, with Santa as the client who didn't pay. Exhibits A–C are the Santa letter, the cookie thank-you note and the magic key. The last slide comes clean: "Santa always pays."

**Caption:**
> Santa, we know you're reading this. 🎅📜 Tag a parent who's been doing Santa's paperwork for years 👇
> Christmas Eve Bundle: letter from Santa, cookie notes, magic key and more. Instant download, link in bio.
>
> #santaletter #christmashumor #momhumor #christmaseve #nicelist #christmasprintables #parentinghumor #etsyfinds
