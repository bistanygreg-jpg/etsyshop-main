# JPEG copies of scheduled TikTok photo posts

TikTok Business photo posts reject PNG ("image/png not allowed, use jpeg"). On Nov 9, 2026 the Follow the Line carousel failed on TikTok for this reason. These are JPEG copies (quality 92) of the PNG media in the 12 Metricool posts scheduled for Nov 10–22: the trend carousels and the TikTok shirt and golf series. Files are named by the original Metricool file id. Archie converted them on Nov 10, and the posts now point at these files.

**Rule for the future:** any image meant for TikTok must be JPEG (or WebP), not PNG.
