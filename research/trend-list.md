# Trend list for Bob's daily Printify drop (Reach, Nov 16, 2026)

**Owner decision (Nov 16):** we've done enough of one Christmas theme. Bob's daily batch now mixes in trend-led and year-round shirts, so different buyers get a chance.

Sources were mostly seller and vendor blogs, not hard Etsy sales data, so treat this as a list of ideas to test:
- [Playground: November 2026 POD trends](https://playgroundai.com/blog/november-2026-print-on-demand-design-trends)
- [Printify: POD niches 2026](https://printify.com/blog/print-on-demand-niches/)
- [MerchOne: Etsy POD niches 2026](https://merchone.com/blog/etsy-print-on-demand-niches/)
- [Merch Titans: best-selling designs](https://merchtitans.com/blog/best-selling-t-shirt-designs)
- [ListerRank: untapped niches](https://getlisterrank.com/blog/8-untapped-print-on-demand-niches-for-etsy-sellers)
- [Etsyhunt: best-selling tees](https://etsyhunt.com/best-etsy-t-shirts)

What they agree on:
- **Specific communities beat broad themes.** Shirts made "for one kind of person" do better than generic quote tees.
- **Humor sells.**
- **For November:** funny Christmas animals, Christmas cookie "club" designs, matching family crew shirts and cozy winter sweatshirts.
- **Crowded categories:** generic "Mama" tees, plain quote tees and bridesmaid shirts.

## Ideas, in build order

All wording must be original. No memes copied from other sellers, celebrities, brands, teams, leagues, characters or trademarked phrases. Check each phrase on Etsy search first: if a big seller owns it word for word, reword it.

## How we'll know

- Hawk compares views and favorites per listing every Monday, trend items against Christmas items.
- Two weeks with no views on a theme: stop building it.
- A theme that gets favorites or a sale: build the matching set (kids, toddler and adult).
