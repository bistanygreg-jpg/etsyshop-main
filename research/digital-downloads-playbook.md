# Digital downloads: Reach's research method and findings (started Nov 16, 2026)

**Owner request (Nov 16):** be thorough in getting insights for digital downloads. Printables are our highest-margin products (no production cost, no shipping) and they can sell right up to Christmas Eve. Reach runs this method every Tuesday, and each weekly brief has a "Digital downloads" section built from it.

## What we know about our own printables (owner's Etsy stats, Nov 16)

- All-time views: Mega Bundle 1, Nice List Kit 1, Wish List 1, Elf Letters 1, Moonlight Tooth Fairy 1, sports award 1. Countdown Kit 2, Scavenger Hunt 2, Lavender Tooth Fairy 2. Zero favorites, zero orders.
- Etsy Ads: the Mega Bundle had 6 ad views and the Countdown Kit 2, with 0 clicks. **The Nice List Kit isn't in the ad set**, although the approved ads test named it.
- Traffic: Etsy Search sent 1 visit in 30 days. So the first job is getting seen in search, not converting visitors.

## Weekly method (every Tuesday brief)

1. **Our numbers first.**
   - Per printable: views, favorites, orders, ad views and clicks (from HQ and Hawk's log).
   - Flag any printable with 0 views for 3 weeks running.
2. **Demand and timing.** Keep a printable season calendar with peak and "list by" dates:
   - Christmas Eve: peak Dec 10–24; list by Nov 15
   - New Year's Eve for kids: peak Dec 20–31; list by Dec 5
   - Valentine's classroom cards: peak late Jan–Feb 13; list by Jan 5
   - 100th day of school: peak mid–late Jan to Feb; list by Jan 5
   - Easter hunt clues: list by Feb 20
   - Tooth Fairy: evergreen
3. **Competitor read, top 5 per product, from web search results only.** Etsy pages are bot-protected, so never scrape them. Record:
   - price
   - bundle size (number of pages)
   - first-photo style (flat vs lifestyle)
   - whether the title leads with "printable" or "instant download"
   - review counts where they appear in search snippets
4. **Community signal.**
   - Reddit (r/EtsySellers, r/Etsy) and Etsy community threads about printables. Reddit is blocked from this environment, so when the owner shares a thread, Reach reads the pasted text and logs the lessons below.
   - Note what sellers say is working *this* season.
5. **Recommendations.**
   - Up to 3 digital actions a week: new product, bundle, photo fix, price or season move. Each one gets an owner agent and a confidence level.
   - Every claim is labeled with its source and confidence. No invented numbers.
